## Frozen scorer and source-first audit

The scorer's exact-quote/JSON validity count was C 2/4, P 2/4, G 1/4,
H 3/4, H-new 2/4. Requiring both validity and the predeclared status gives
C 1/4, P 2/4, G 1/4, H 1/4, H-new 2/4. These are descriptive diagnostics,
not efficacy estimates. Ten of 20 answers gave a semantically nearby but
non-exact source quote, often flattening source line breaks; they fail the
frozen output contract.

The decisive treatment failure is independent of those scores: all four
semantic-preparation outputs contained at least one quote that was not an
exact substring of the supplied source. The v1 source boundary rejected all
four preparations. Every H answer therefore had **zero checked explanation
candidates** and, for every case, the final H and H-new messages were
**byte-for-byte equal**. The ablation cannot attribute any gain to the new
condition mechanism. A post-run diagnostic found additional candidate
attribution failures: some model-extracted candidate source spans did not
name their target actor. This was not repaired or rescored within the paid
run.

The source-first review found further limitations. On *The Necklace*
invitation, P gave the predeclared invalidation while H returned unresolved.
On the dress offer, C/P/H/H-new returned the conditional status, without a
specialized gain. Every hair-sale arm failed the exact-quote contract; H
also mentioned a dollar amount absent from the supplied excerpt. On the
combs question all arms returned invalidated against the predeclared
unresolved condition, often treating Jim's later reaction as proof of his
knowledge at purchase. That case's semantic interpretation is delicate and
cannot support a strong comparative claim. No unique private motive is
established. The reviewer inspected arm labels during diagnosis, so this is
not an independent arm-masked efficacy audit.

## Decision and limits

**SIMPLIFY** is the Phase E deployment decision for this ordinary-text
explanation class. P was cheaper and at least as useful in this run; H
did not provide the promised actual checker treatment, and it cost more.
This decision does not establish that CG-01's deterministic condition checker
is ineffective. It preserves the provider-free correctness result and keeps
the checker available only when source-validated typed inputs are present.
No source-only label, test count, or post-run code repair is treated as
external efficacy.

The generic provider-free repair after this run aligns only uniquely matching
whitespace variants to exact source spans, drops unanchored events and their
dependent claims, derives event order from source position, rejects unsupported
first-learning assertions and candidate attributions, and preserves raw
semantic output/cost on validation failure. These changes are prospective;
they do not alter this run's scores. The one-time paid workflow grant is
closed at zero. Any new external comparison needs a separately frozen package
and owner authorization.
