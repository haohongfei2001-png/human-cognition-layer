# G01 — Source-origin normative premise candidates

## CAPABILITY_DELTA

An ordinary responsibility question now yields explicit, typed **conditional
premise candidates** without asking the caller to fill `FactorRequirement`
objects. HCL separates a rule supplied in the actual user question, an
explicitly adopted analyst framework, a source-reported institution policy,
and a person's source-reported endorsement. A generic responsibility question
can receive one clearly unadopted analyst proposal. Only completely parsed
user-supplied or explicitly adopted conditions can become CG03
`NarrativePremise` objects. Source statements and automatic proposals cannot
silently become the analyst's rule or moral truth.

The simplest alternative is a prompt asking the base model to list possible
rules. Serious independent evaluation must test whether the origin and
adoption boundary improves real answers enough to retain the operation.

## Mechanism and limits

`NormativePremiseWorkspace` reads bounded ordinary questions and authorized
source lines. It preserves exact source quote, offset, version, ACL and
event/access/record cutoffs. The grammar accepts small explicit conjunctive
responsibility conditions over the retained CG03 factors: causal contribution,
knowledge, foreseeability, control and stated intention. Disjunction,
exception, negation, unknown terms or duplicate factors stay unresolved; a
partial parse is not turned into a runnable rule. Source-reported character
endorsement does not establish a private value or actual acceptance. A
reported institution policy does not establish a valid law or obligation.

The automatically proposed knowledge/control framework is labelled
`ANALYST_PROPOSED_UNADOPTED`. It is a candidate for analysis, not a hidden
default. A caller must supply or explicitly adopt a rule before the typed
CG03 bridge is available. G01 does not assess responsibility; G02 will connect
factors and conditional conclusions at action time.
