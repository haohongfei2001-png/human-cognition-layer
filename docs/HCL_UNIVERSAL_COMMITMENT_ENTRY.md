# Retained D01 conditional commitment in ordinary entry

Ordinary HCL planning can select the existing D01 lifecycle for one complete
source using `What is the status of <Speaker>'s promise to <Recipient> to <action>?`
The two actors must be distinct source-named single tokens. Shared communication
requires ASCII names of at most 32 characters, starting uppercase, followed by
letters, digits, underscore or hyphen; pronouns and Narrator are not actors.
Action is at most 200 characters and the operation question at most 8,000.
The original outer request remains unchanged. Actor bindings are unused.

The adapter invokes the native preparer on the shared workspace, validates its
current messages and forwards its entire payload, policy and every support claim
ID. Checked treatment requires CHECKED_CONDITIONAL_LIFECYCLE. A missing, negative
or hypothetical promise can remain SYSTEM_INSUFFICIENT with no treatment; an
unsupported operation is not repaired into a promise. Other outer sources remain
in final input but cannot supply facts to the selected single-source lifecycle.

## Separate lifecycle and access reports

The original conditional promise, condition evidence, addressing, receipt,
acceptance, expectation, withdrawal and reported fulfillment remain separate.
Missing condition evidence is unknown, not false. Source contradictions remain
explicit. Acceptance does not establish receipt or private comprehension.
Later receipt changes current access without backfilling an earlier expectation.
Withdrawal preserves the earlier promise and expectation history. Reported
fulfillment is not a verified outcome, and no moral obligation, blame or trust
verdict follows. Source order is an assumption, not verified chronology.

The B02 prerequisite guards the exact source scope of cues, their literal targets
and own expressions. Shared support includes every retained source read dependency,
including negative/conflicting reports; revision guards also cover absent facts.
Withdrawal before answering blocks admission, and changes during answer discard
delivery. Native D01 and B02 code are unchanged by this adapter.

## Existing limits and full transport

Native limits remain one matching conditional promise, 24 accepted source rows,
six social acts, two withdrawals and two expectations for that promise. The shared
64-candidate A02 cap can bind first: the authored 21-line/62-candidate source is
accepted while its 22-line/65-candidate extension is refused without truncation.
Communication requires one complete unambiguous statement or access cue on each
nonblank line, at most 80 lines, 4,000 characters per line, 64,000 source characters
and eight actors. These are maximum bounds, not promises that every input below
one bound satisfies all the others.

The four-line native fixture calls shared workspace semantic preparation once,
and separately prepares two B02 prefixes for current access and expectation-time
access. These are existing local syntax operations, not semantic provider calls.
This adapter does not migrate caches or change parsing costs.

The scripted citation/lifecycle witness begins with nonreceipt and an expectation,
then appends later receipt and withdrawal. Old support becomes unsupported; the
new current view records receipt/withdrawal while the earlier expectation retains
its nonreceipt. The source citation keeps its original quote, offset and current
revision; quotation location is verified and semantic correctness is unassessed.
Complete request sizes are 23,780 bytes planning and 8,221 answering before revision,
and 23,906/9,412 afterward. Planning request hashes identify the recorded run,
including its actual ingestion timestamp; they are not timeless replay hashes.
The unchanged 36,000-byte transport still governs.
An oversized three-operation native result blocks answer invocation while keeping
all source, policy and operation data. Oversized planning input makes no backend
call. No component silently trims evidence to pass a bound.

The amendment preserves 138 historical pins and proves that removing only the
five catalog and nine dispatch lines restores the prior runtime byte-for-byte.
No native grammar, communication scope, provider settings, budget or historical
receipt is changed. This brings bounded ordinary adapters to thirteen of forty
retained packages. Live model selection and answer efficacy remain unverified;
B04 complete-evidence transport is still a separate unresolved gate. All paid
grants remain closed and this source work makes zero provider calls.
