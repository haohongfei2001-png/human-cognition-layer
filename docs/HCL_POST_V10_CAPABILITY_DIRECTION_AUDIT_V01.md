## Candidate choice: contextual indirect-answer interpretation

Communication understanding is a direct social cognition capability. It benefits from separating literal text, context-supported implication, conditions and reader uncertainty, while preserving source grounding. [PaCE (ACL 2026)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.959/) reports context-dependent overinterpretation and improvement from strict prompting; this motivates testing a simple method and a strong direct control. It does not establish a gap in the current deepseek-flash alias. No public PaCE dataset pin was located in the inspected paper/search; paper examples are development-exposed. Do not recreate its examples and claim external fresh efficacy.

[CEI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09993) is a licensed multi-annotator contextual emotion dataset. Its target is emotion attribution, with acknowledged ambiguous annotation, not direct pragmatic interpretation. No row or gold was read; do not reopen the closed affect direction using these labels as true intentions. [Hu et al.'s author repo](https://github.com/jennhu/lm-pragmatics) at 9fd4be5c0e43162c9d6e09b6024cc000e40b3398 was inspected at README/repository metadata only, without source prompts or outcomes. PUB metadata/repository file lists inspected; Hugging Face metadata request timed out once, so no repeated stalled fetch. Neither source is used as a substitute scored dataset here.

[Circa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03450) supplies directly relevant crowd-authored polar questions, indirect replies and independent human interpretations. Pin google-research-datasets/circa at 02ad965518ab2fbd8bb24463d312ebb03bac5368, full TSV SHA-256 98454df6b716dd7ff5f83a3db298849f05414688e81c2ee21b8e5a548ed897aa (34,268 rows). README/paper-abstract examples and source header, unique contexts and global label vocabulary were inspected; selected case annotations and source text were not displayed. Exclude entire food/music contexts and exposed reply fragments “go to bed” and “starving”. The remaining eight contexts yield exactly one source-only-selected dialogue each, frozen before provider calls, gold values never used for selection. This is an initial development method screen, not fresh efficacy.
