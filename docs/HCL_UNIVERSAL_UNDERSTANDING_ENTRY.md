# Retained D02 finite acknowledgment in ordinary entry

The existing D02 preparer can now be selected for one complete source using
`Do <Speaker> and <Recipient> share an acknowledged understanding that <content>?`
The native question requires distinct capitalized single-token participants,
content at most 250 characters and question at most 8,000. The operation interprets
the unchanged outer request; it cannot invent an interpretation, confirmation or
receipt. Actor bindings are unused. All outer sources remain in final composition.

After native messages validate against the shared workspace, the adapter obtains
the target only from this result's supported MUTUAL_UNDERSTANDING_SUMMARY claim.
Treatment requires exact content equality with one returned acknowledgment chain
or explicit doubt. It does not scan unrelated workspace claims, reparse the
question, normalize content or infer treatment from a status/summary alone.
An unrelated chain or bare historical meaning therefore remains untreated.

## Finite links, history and negative evidence

Original receipt at interpretation, interpretation receipt at confirmation and
current receipt of the confirmation remain three separate links. A supported
incomplete chain remains incomplete. Later receipt cannot backfill an earlier
link. Speaker confirmation cannot be replaced by a third-party statement, an
"Okay" or a higher-order belief report. Source order is not verified chronology.

An explicit revision preserves the earlier acknowledgment as historical and does
not automatically update the other participant. A new meaning without a chain
remains unacknowledged. Matching explicit doubt is negative source-report evidence,
not a positive acknowledgment or a validated receipt chain. No private
comprehension, infinite common knowledge or automatic trust restoration follows.
The full native payload/policy and every support claim ID remain unchanged.

Source revision and withdrawal invalidate shared support. Admission rechecks it
before answering and discards delivery if source/support changes during answer.
The native preparer, B02 communication authority and all parsers are unchanged.

## Source format and branch-specific bounds

This adapter accepts LF/CRLF and refuses all other Python splitlines separators
before native preparation, retaining exact original source bytes. Native D02
counts LF for receipt lookups while B02 recognizes a wider set; the mismatch can
otherwise turn unrelated own speech into a false acknowledgment. The standalone
native format gap remains unrepaired, and B02's wider support remains unchanged.

Native preparation retains the 64-candidate semantic and 20 accepted-row limits,
plus 64,000 source characters, 80 nonblank lines and 4,000 characters per line.
Receipt-bearing views additionally require complete unambiguous lines, actual
cue/target authority, ASCII actor names of at most 32 characters and eight actors.
Those view checks are lazy. A doubt-only native report may exist without invoking
a receipt view, including broader report-channel names or line forms. Such a
report does not certify a valid receipt identity or actual private understanding.
The descriptor keeps this branch distinction rather than inventing stricter
native query behavior. Complete requests must fit the unchanged 36,000-byte bound.

## Complete composition witness

The D01/D02 scripted witness keeps the conditional promise's nonreceipt separate
from the later acknowledgment of a different reported meaning. Initially D02 has
a finite chain; after a meaning revision, that chain is historical. Querying the
new meaning produces no chain and treatment=false. Old support becomes unsupported
and both original citations retain exact quotes, offsets and current versions.
Citation location is checked; semantic correctness and model selection remain
unassessed.

Recorded complete requests measure 25,789/12,942 bytes planning/answering initially,
25,884/13,694 for the historical target after revision, and 25,884/13,707 for the
new target without a chain. Planning hashes include actual ingestion timestamps.
An oversized composition retains three complete native histories and operations
but stops before answer; oversized planning input makes no backend call.

The 147-pin amendment preserves prior runtime/evidence and proves that removing
only five catalog and thirteen dispatch lines restores the certified base.
This brings bounded ordinary adapters to fourteen of forty retained packages;
it does not complete capability integration or establish live answer efficacy.
B04 complete-evidence transport and native non-LF format support remain separate
gaps. No paid calls, new grants, parser changes or historical rescoring are added.
