# HCL validation policy — two separate evidence tracks

Status: CANONICAL OWNER POLICY, 2026-10-01. Supersedes the global daily-development
block caused by I02 protected confirmation source / independent-reviewer availability.
Historical runtime, receipt, disposition, consumed grants and I01 freeze stay immutable.

## 1. Development Reality Checks — active now

Purpose: learn whether ordinary current HCL helps a strong base on real target tasks,
and use observed errors, missing coverage, reverse harm and absent gains to improve,
simplify or remove mechanisms. A small negative result is useful development evidence.

A new batch requires concrete items not previously exposed in HCL development,
calibration, screening or historical runs; substantively relevant tasks; legally
available public development-test material; same underlying model and fair complete
ordinary input for Base/HCL; and a subset/model/prompt/scorer freeze before execution.
Record the provenance/history check's actual scope; do not claim model-pretraining
novelty or exhaustive deleted-ref independence. Metadata discovery is not a final
confirmation qualification. Record every item actually opened and quarantine its
source/test system from final confirmation immediately.

Start with **strong Base vs current HCL**. P/G, independent reviewer, three source
systems, ablation, cross-family transfer, blind review and final statistical power
are not prerequisites for the first development check. Keep meaningful native model
reasoning enabled and charge all H preparation/retrieval/model work. Preserve full
source, native question, choices and answer vocabulary; do not inject gold, correct
mental state, hand-selected mechanism route or answer hints. Report direct/refusal/
missing-treatment outcomes instead of quietly dropping them or treating absent H
mechanism as an experiment-execution gate.

Freeze seed/subset/item hashes, native labels, common answer contract, model settings,
current runtime/execution hashes, costs/call ceilings and scoring before responses.
Keep full requests/responses, returned model IDs, usage, latency, preparation state,
all attempted/failed cases, coverage, paired accuracy and reverse harm. Native-label
agreement is a benchmark metric, not verified private intention or moral truth.
Ambiguous or questionable gold is documented separately; the frozen primary score
is not revised after seeing arm outcomes. Optional implementer review stays labeled.

Once a question, answer or result is viewed, label the material
**DEVELOPMENT_EXPOSED / CONSUMED_FOR_FINAL_CONFIRMATION**. It may support debugging
and regression; any repeat is explicitly a consumed-development regression, never
fresh evidence. No silent automatic rerun, case substitution or reused grant. After
using a batch, choose new unexposed items/test sets for the next reality check.
No single-item/template/benchmark-specific runtime patches; fix general evidenced
failures. Do not redesign H around a prospective final confirmation source.

## 2. Final Sealed Confirmation — separate, later

Only after capabilities and operational settings are sufficiently frozen, collect
and protect confirmation material isolated from *all* development benchmarks and
related exposed source/writing systems. Apply strict source independence, competent
blinded semantic review, strong Base/P/G comparators, genuine matched ablation,
cross-model transfer when available, clustered statistics and explicit evidence
scope. Existing I01 and I02 frozen final-evaluation contracts retain their strict
meaning; they govern final claims, not daily development admission.

Development positives, synthetic fixtures, local tests, implementer review and
native label agreement cannot become final independent evidence by renaming a row,
adding a reviewer attestation, switching an arm or calling a later run “fresh.”
Use the development exposure firewall in addition to all historical final source
gates. Absence of an ideal final source or independent reviewer never blocks an
otherwise eligible Development Reality Check or general capability repair.

## 3. Execution priority and boundaries

Current queue: **DRC001_FRESH_BASE_VS_HCL_REALITY_CHECK**, then source-first development
closure and general error/coverage repairs, then a fresh development batch. I02
final qualification is deferred as a *final* prerequisite, not a global stop.
Existing low-cost provider authorization remains bounded per new package; no old
budget transfer, new account/credential/plan or major legal/privacy commitment.
One writer / one coherent PR; exact-head CI → merge → exact-main CI.
Leaderboard remains off until the original maturity gate. LongMemEval stays
**SEALED / DEPRIORITIZED / NOT ACCESSED**. The architecture and evidence boundaries
remain; this policy changes validation cadence, not historical conclusions.
