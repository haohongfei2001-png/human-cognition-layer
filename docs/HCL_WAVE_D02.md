# D02 — Bounded mutual understanding and repair

## CAPABILITY_DELTA

HCL distinguishes two people having heard a statement from an explicitly received
interpretation/confirmation chain. An ambiguous “Okay” establishes neither. When
the speaker revises the meaning with an exact prior anchor, the old acknowledgment
becomes historical; a new acknowledgment requires a new received chain. No private
comprehension, infinite common knowledge or restored trust is inferred.

`prepare_mutual_understanding` connects source meaning → recipient's reported
interpretation → speaker confirmation → recipient's receipt. B02 checks each
receipt at the relevant formation time, preventing later delivery from backfilling
an earlier report. A01/A02 ground every step; B01 retains explicit higher-order
mental expressions without detaching inner beliefs. Contradictory or uncertain
understanding reports suspend positive acknowledgment. A confirmation with multiple
possible interpretation anchors remains unresolved. The current source projection
and historical meanings/acknowledgments are separately available in final input.

## Verification and limits

Thirteen targeted checks cover the positive chain, exposure/ambiguous response,
missing final receipt, later-delivery non-backfill, explicit revision and new chain,
uncertainty, actor/source/access boundaries, higher-order attribution, bad revision
anchors, source/support invalidation and budget. Full v1 and 176 historical
regressions run in exact-head/main CI. `scripts/witness_mutual_understanding.py`
saves actual positive final inputs in `reports/HCL_WAVE_D02_WITNESS.json`.
