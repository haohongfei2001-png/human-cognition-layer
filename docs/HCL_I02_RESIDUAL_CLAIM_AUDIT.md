# I02 residual-claim audit supplement

Status: **PROVIDER-FREE REVIEW INTERFACE; NO INDEPENDENT SEMANTIC QUALIFICATION**.
The frozen v1 source-first obligations and historical scores are unchanged.
The [supplemental checker](../scripts/i02_residual_claim_audit.py) accepts a
complete v1 review and a second, arm-blind record of material assertions in
the answer that the fixed obligations may miss. Every listed assertion needs
an exact answer excerpt, a reasoned support judgment and, when judged
supported, an exact authorized-source quotation. Severe unsupported actor,
time, access, private-state, normative or permission claims and unresolved
residual claims prevent eligibility. A perfect v1 fraction therefore cannot
alone establish a complete answer is source-grounded.

The positive synthetic witness preserves the original 11/11 score and
records a supported reported goal. The negative witness adds an ungrounded
private intention to the same otherwise perfect answer: v1 remains 11/11,
while the supplemental result is ineligible. Fake source quotations,
duplicate/nonexistent output excerpts, missing coverage attestation and
allocation mismatches fail closed. This composes with the existing v1
source and answer validation; old frozen receipts are not rescored or
rewritten.

**EVALUATION CAPABILITY_DELTA:** reviewers can now record and disqualify a
severe unsupported assertion outside the case's prespecified obligations.
Previously such an answer could receive full v1 credit. **HCL cognition
CAPABILITY_DELTA:** none. The software cannot prove that a human listed *all*
material claims, was truly independent, or judged their semantics correctly.
The developer-blind ACL v8 scores remain development diagnostics and are not
retrospectively upgraded. A genuinely independent reviewer and unseen,
rights-clear sources remain required before I03.
