# E01–E02 — Scoped relationship evidence and conflict repair

## CAPABILITY_DELTA — E01

Regard stays directional and specific to domain/aspect. Competence trust does not
become honesty trust, a reverse view or a score. A cited reason's supporting and
contradicting source evidence can change while the reported attitude remains
unchanged. Only an explicit revision with a matching prior stance retires that
local report. Third-party attribution remains distinct from direct expression.

`prepare_relationship` uses A01/A02 source grounding and keeps source-basis checks
separate from expressed regard. Earlier versus current source basis is explicit;
source order does not establish the assessor's access. Unsupported reasons stay
self-reported reasons. No actual trait or private attitude is established.

## CAPABILITY_DELTA — E02

Failure can be conditionally explained by information gaps, control constraints,
or an informed/controllable stated choice. A changed factor report updates these
alternatives without rewriting a separate relationship judgment. Receiving or
accepting an apology differs from an explicit forgiveness report; neither proves
private forgiveness or sincerity.

`prepare_relational_conflict` consumes E01 relationship evidence, B02 apology
receipt and CG03's asserted-factor polarity/aggregation helpers. Exact attempt,
action and domain anchors precede knowledge/control/intention conversion. Original
quotes and explicitly derived factor clauses are retained. Intention to succeed is
not intention to fail; later learning is not action-time knowledge. This is factor
comparison, with no manufactured normative premise or responsibility case.

The simplest alternative is a generic table of source judgments, reasons and
failure conditions. Serious post-G-ARCH comparison should simplify these joins
if their bounded revision and cross-operation handling add no utility.

## Verification and limits

witnesses, source/actor/domain/aspect/direction/access/time boundaries, uncertainty,
negative/refusal cases, local revision, counterevidence, actual final inputs,
composition, support invalidation and budgets. Full v1 and 176 historical
regressions run in exact-head/main CI. Separate ordinary positive witnesses save
actual inputs in `reports/HCL_WAVE_E01_WITNESS.json` and
`reports/HCL_WAVE_E02_WITNESS.json`. Preceding D05 exact-main
`1990fb07d30431ac04f125f974a3a49eb0b5bc95` passed all six workflows (v1
`36494270801`).

Both are **CORRECTNESS_VERIFIED / REPLAY_VERIFIED / UNTESTED / OPT_IN**. Bounded
explicit English, one relationship query and one failure episode, at most 20 source
statements subject to A02 candidate limits. Source accuracy/sincerity and ordinal
time remain assumptions. No global trust/personality graph, moral blame, actual
forgiveness or independent efficacy claim. Zero provider calls/spend; LongMemEval
sealed. NEXT_READY: E03 self-narrative, role and identity boundaries.
