## Educational philosophy source and questions

The [TRU Social Contract Theory chapter](https://introductiontoethicsversion2.pressbooks.tru.ca/chapter/social-contract-theory/)
declares CC BY 4.0 except where noted and explicitly credits adaptation of
the [Rebus Ya-Yun Kao chapter](https://press.rebus.community/intro-to-phil-ethics/chapter/whats-in-it-for-me-on-egoism-and-social-contract-theory/),
whose original chapter also declares CC BY 4.0 except where noted. Both
chapter prose and their question wording were visible to the implementer.
The TRU chapter's four discussion questions include opinion or contemporary
application, and one refers to an outside story absent from the same
authorized source. A chapter notice therefore does not make all four
questions source-bounded I01 abstract concept witnesses. The original Rebus
chapter asks a central philosophical question inside its own explanation,
but it has no separate native evaluation item or source-first answer audit.
Neither chapter was sent to a model or promoted to protected confirmation.

case-specific attribution says **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, despite a CC BY notice
on a different chapter. Paid-provider use and exception scope were not
cleared. The case is development-exposed and blocked from model input.
