# C01 — Source-grounded goals, means and plans

## CAPABILITY_DELTA

Ordinary first-person dialogue now distinguishes an active goal, a considered
means and an explicitly selected plan. Joining selected-plan evidence with the
matching goal and reported opportunity can positively support pursuit, without
claiming world feasibility or success. Explicitly abandoning the goal changes the
dependent pursuit result without inventing plan abandonment. Completing a subgoal
does not complete its parent; merely achieving an outcome does not establish
intention or goal completion.

## Mechanism and reuse

`prepare_agency` binds ordinary source dialogue through A02 and A01. Explicit goal
transitions run through retained v0.7 evidence validation/projection. New scoped
relations distinguish subgoal-for, considered/selected plan, plan lifecycle,
condition, opportunity and intention. Conditional join claims depend on the actual
plan, goal and opportunity support nodes. Goal uncertainty remains explicit even
where the retained thin projector preserves an earlier active state.

The question identifies the focal actor inside one source identity domain. Observer
filtering occurs before extraction, and B02 projected past source snapshots can be
used directly. v0.7 adapter timestamps encode source order only, visibly; they are
not invented calendar facts. A source correction invalidates prepared results.
Actual final inputs preserve original source, dependency receipts, native retained
state and new conditional plan checks.

Statements such as `I plan to cross the bridge in order to reach shelter if the
gate is open` retain the condition without assuming it holds. No opportunity is
not the same as abandoning the goal. Competing opportunity reports stay conflicting.
Plan lifecycle references require one matching prior plan; ambiguous/missing ones
remain diagnostics. Private intention, causation and success are never promoted
from an action or a resulting outcome.
