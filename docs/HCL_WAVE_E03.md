# E03 — Self-narrative, identity and role boundaries

## CAPABILITY_DELTA

HCL compares a person's self-description with another person's attribution,
source-local role occupancy, declared requirements and reported behavior. It can
identify a behavior/requirement tension without changing the person's identity or
assuming endorsement. An explicit self-description revision replaces only its
anchored prior report; leaving a role preserves historical behavior-time checks.

`prepare_identity_roles` reuses shared evidence and semantic preparation. The
positive ordinary-text witness shows a self-described careful reviewer, another
person's careless attribution, a missed check and explicit rejection of the local
requirement. All channels and their original source support enter the final model
input. Role/rule evidence appearing after behavior cannot backfill that check.

The simplest alternative is a generic source table. Simplify this mechanism if
post-G-ARCH evaluation finds no gain from its temporal joins and scoped revision.
No global personality, hypocrisy, moral truth or private endorsement is inferred.
