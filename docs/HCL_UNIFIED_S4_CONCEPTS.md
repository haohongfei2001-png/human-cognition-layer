# S4 structural concepts: implementation in progress

This bounded offline implementation advances approved M12 and its M11 consumer.
It does not close M11/M12/S4 or T13–T16/T27. The pending predecessor sequence,
exact published migration pins, independent reviews and publication gates remain.

## Executed computation

The ordinary authenticated `analogy.compare` graph accepts source-anchored typed
proposals, never solved correspondences, transfer verdicts, induced patterns or
beauty/confidence scores. The same operator is callable inside the narrative
reading path. Every base/target entity, predicate, relation, compatibility and
example retains original source spans. Questions and remembered intertexts are
not evidence. Semantic alignment remains a hypothesis.

The mapping solver constructs local argument-preserving matches, joins only
consistent one-to-one entity/predicate/fact assignments, and enumerates bounded
partial mappings. Higher-order relation arguments constrain the same underlying
assignments; a shared label does not establish a match. Unary attributes do not
seed analogies. Explicit attribute contradictions remain secondary dissimilarity
checks. Exact duplicate assertions cannot manufacture extra structural support.

Each candidate projects only relations whose required entity and predicate
bindings are established. It checks these against both positive and explicit
negative target assertions, retaining supported, refuted, conflicting and
undetermined cases. Every projection remains hypothetical even when the declared
target model supports it. Missing target assertions are not negative evidence.
Per-prediction evidence and assumptions, and the separate conditions and source
spans for each ordering metric, accompany the final claim. Metrics collect every
actually contributing projection, including facts not yet joined into a bounded
search-prefix mapping. A nested ATTRIBUTE can establish a predicate binding;
secondary checks obey that binding before considering unbound unique compatibility.
An unbound compatibility cannot reuse a target predicate already assigned to a
different base predicate; that check stays explicitly unresolved.
Negative attribute evidence remains visible but never also penalizes the primary
relational score.

Higher-order expressions use a content-addressed FACT DAG: each flat node names
its predicate, polarity and ordered entity/child-node references, and each mapping
retains the complete projected node table plus each hypothesis root. Child nodes
are shared through hashing, freezing, serialization, persistence and delivery;
there is no implicit expansion into an exponential tree. Depth is the true
subtree height, independent of input fact ordering. Validation visits at most 32
nodes and 128 argument edges per domain, outside the charged search-work count.

The structural ordering is explicit: higher-order links, then relational support
minus explicit RELATION counterexamples, then attribute mismatches. It is a computational
comparison of the declared finite structures, not a probability, psychological
measurement, guarantee of semantic usefulness or aesthetic ranking. Unsearched
alternatives and incomplete maximality are retained as frontiers.

The induction component actually anti-unifies finite ground typed terms from
positive examples. Repeated disagreement vectors become the same generated
variable, preserving correlations such as pair(x,x), rather than independently
replacing each occurrence. It then matches the resulting pattern against each
positive and negative/boundary example. A matching negative case is an explicit
counterexample, not discarded training data. Withdrawn example assumptions and
uncomputed coverage remain visible. This is first-order syntactic term
anti-unification, not unrestricted clause/rule or causal induction.

Each proposed concept interpretation retains its pragmatic purpose, source
examples, conditions, stable scoped family identity and content-addressed version.
A changed interpretation appends a new inference; earlier task objects and
versions remain immutable. Automatic semantic concept-family identity, a complete
version-dependency registry, integration of revisions into all old commitments,
and M02/M13/M14 consumer propagation remain unfinished.

## Narrative and durable integration

Narrative fragments may propose locally source-bound structural tasks. The actual
M12 mapping/generalization computation consumes the same remaining task allowance
after story/form and M04 comparisons. Its exact input, evidence, conditions,
counterexamples and limitations are checked again before compact delivery. Both
base and target must be present in the packet: cross-packet structural assembly
and unsupported remembered intertext are not silently filled in.

Actual persisted RETURNED, computed and committed states resume without new
model sends or recomputing accepted effects. UNKNOWN sends retain reservations.
Fresh cached ACL checks, commit identities, governed erasure dependencies and
existing delivery byte limits still apply. Injected offline proposals are test
conditions, not evidence of open-model reading or extraction accuracy.

## Bounds and open research

Mapping domains permit at most 16 entities, 16 predicates and 32 relation instances
per side, four arguments, and 16 levels of acyclic relation expressions. The
solver charges local-match checks, recursive constraints, search states, bounded
leaf/transfer evaluations and maximality comparisons. Search is capped at one
million work units per model, 8,192 visited search states, 4,096 saved candidates and 64 delivered mappings in
the source-bound API. These are algorithmic bounds, not CPU-time accounting.
Induction permits 16 positive and 32 negative terms, at most 256 nodes and depth
16 per term. All existing context, source, output and aggregate task bounds apply.
No exhausted search certifies completeness, maximality, absence or a learned law.

Still open: unrestricted concept generation, equivalence beyond the typed
syntactic language, counterexample construction in open target worlds, fully
shared concept-version revision across consumers, calibrated transfer reliability,
whole-work metaphor/irony interpretation and the approved multi-reviewer
natural-language research acceptance. The broader M11 character, rhetoric,
form-effect and aesthetic obligations are unchanged.

## Method attribution

Relational correspondence and argument-preserving structural consistency follow
the research tradition of [Gentner's structure-mapping theory](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/Gentner83.2b.pdf)
and [Falkenhainer, Forbus and Gentner's SME](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/FalkenhainerForbusGentner89.pdf).
This code uses its own bounded enumeration and explicit ordering; it is not SME,
not a replication of its empirical results, and not claimed as an original
structure-mapping theory.

The typed term generalizer uses the syntactic anti-unification tradition described
by [Plotkin](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/gdp/publications/MI6_further_note.pdf).
Its finite example checks do not establish an unrestricted empirical law.
