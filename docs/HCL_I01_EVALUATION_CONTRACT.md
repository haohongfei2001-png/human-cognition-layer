## Question and observable outcome

Does the **complete frozen HCL**, from ordinary question plus authorized source
text through extraction, route, retrieval, checks, revision and final answer,
improve source-bounded human-cognition answers over the best qualified strong
base (C), strong prompt/process (P), and competent generic scaffold (G)? Is any
benefit due to cognition mechanisms or their integration, and is it worth full
cost and reverse harm? A refusal, unsupported inference, access leak, wrong
actor/time/source, or inability to parse an ordinary question is an observed
outcome, not a reason to silently remove the case.

The four fixed families are long character development; multiparty information
and strategy; responsibility with explicit value or norm premises; and abstract
concept/philosophy analysis. At least three independent source/writing systems
and two independent model families are the maturity target. If source or model
coverage is smaller, claims are restricted to the observed scope. A case is
independent by origin, author, structure and writing system, not by renamed
characters. HCL-authored fixtures and historical provider cases remain
development evidence only. LongMemEval is excluded and sealed.

## Fair execution surface

Every C/P/G/H run starts from the **same ordinary question and authorized
source text** and must return the same open answer contract: `answer`,
`source_citations`, `uncertainty`, `assumptions`. No HCL-private label, hidden
mental-state gold, oracle source segmentation, preselected mechanism route or
post-hoc selected winner may enter an arm's input. Each arm may perform its own
reasonable processing. C receives the then-current strong native configuration
with a reasonable reasoning budget. P receives independently calibrated
prompt/process support. G receives competent generic extraction, retrieval,
memory, dependency maintenance and planning, with enough computation to be a
real alternative. H includes all preparation, routing and final model costs.
Attribution comparisons must share suitable semantic extraction and generic
resources; they may remove a declared cognition mechanism or flatten
integration, but may not remove source access, the answer contract, or working
generic methods. A computation-matched comparator is required before attributing
gain to specialized cognition.

Qualification happens in I02, before viewing confirmation outcomes. The
evaluation question, families and safety boundaries are frozen now; exact
model IDs, calibrated prompts, G implementation, scorer rubric, sample size,
budget and latency bands must be frozen after I02 and **before** confirmation.
Source groups, authors, templates and writing systems cannot cross calibration
and confirmation. Confirmation material is held away from implementers until
this freeze and calibration are complete. Difficulty is chosen by task demands
and C/P/G calibration, never by H wins on confirmation. A source with unclear
rights or unequal access is rejected; no account, credential or private-owner
material is presumed available.

## Measurement and disposition

Primary measurement is blinded, source-audited semantic success on paired
questions, with clustering by independent source/writing system. Record valid
answer coverage, necessary inferences, calibrated uncertainty, critical
actor/source/time/access errors, refusals, reverse harm, token and tool cost,
latency and raw traces. For philosophy, use a prespecified acceptable-reasoning
set and severe-error rubric, not agreement with one moral conclusion. Distinguish
end-to-end utility from mechanism and integrated-composition attribution.

The maturity targets and source-first RETAIN/SIMPLIFY/REDESIGN/INCONCLUSIVE
rules are those of Master Plan §11.3–11.4. No target is achieved by this
freeze. An I02 calibration failure must be repaired before I03; a result from
inadequate C/P/G, contaminated sources, missing treatment or a scorer changed
after confirmation is **INCONCLUSIVE**, never a positive HCL claim.

## Executable guard and limits

`scripts/serious_eval_contract.py` validates the immutable freeze and rejects
candidate cases with authored or unlicensed sources, unequal inputs, HCL-only
answer labels, oracle state, LongMemEval use, and split leakage. Its positive
four-family witness is synthetic and tests only the guard. It does **not**
establish source independence, source rights, model qualification or efficacy;
I02 must inspect actual provenance and freeze the remaining operational details.

**EVALUATION_DELTA:** before I01, a nominal five-arm comparison could quietly
mix inputs or recycle authored cases. The new guard makes those conditions
explicit failures before any provider call. **CAPABILITY_DELTA:** none claimed;
G-ARCH requires evaluation rather than adding another cognition module.
