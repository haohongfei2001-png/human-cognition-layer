## Capability and direction

Conceptual/philosophical reasoning requires distinguishing “each has some” from “there is one for all,” names from quantified individuals, and failing to refute from proving necessity. New `hcl/quantified_logic.py` parses bounded symbolic formulas, evaluates explicit finite interpretations, constructs independently verified finite counterexamples with Z3 and checks full first-order formula equivalence. It supplies an auditable generic evidence instrument; it does not translate NL, know hidden intentions or certify empirical/moral truth. No LLM cognition efficacy follows from solver correctness.
