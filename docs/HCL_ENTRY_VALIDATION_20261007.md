## Rules fixed before these outputs

The original source, ten obligations and four smoke semantic checks are unchanged.
The additional routing rule is separate: no selected operation may match an entry
blocker declared in this package for that exact capability, source ID and version.
Every case additionally requires an executed, checked treatment in its originally
allowed native family. This last Boolean is only a necessary condition; independent
source-first review still has to establish faithful arguments, relevant treatment,
correct attribution/time/uncertainty and the original obligations. A missing blocker
is not a promise of readiness. Other native execution, reader execution counts or
an apparently good final answer do not satisfy the treatment requirement.

The executor never deletes or replaces a selected operation and does not pre-run a
tool. It preserves the original bounded round, actual arguments/results, final
answer and failure evidence. After the round, any detected routing/treatment,
publication-binding or delivery failure stops further cases without retry. Partial
native capture cannot certify all selected operations. The existing full native
record source-anchor, replay and disclosure checks also run after the round and
before any next-case dispatch. A rejected native block retains safe original
final/usage facts and private capture, while its raw rejected fields stay private. Stage 1 cannot release
stage 2 until the independent review and exact phase gate pass. Stage 2 results
also require source-first review; automated necessary conditions do not certify
semantic quality.

## Time, disclosure and limits

There is no fixed calendar cutoff. Stage windows remain 600 / 1,200 seconds, with
180-second request waits and remaining-window dispatch margins. No retry,
replanning, automatic configuration upgrade, replacement case, recharge,
subscription or paid judge is allowed. A stopped experiment cannot spend its
unused budget on another attempt.

At most three final answers and three structured synthetic native records may be
published to `haohongfei2001-png/human-cognition-layer`; each native record is
limited to three operations and 1 MiB. Records bind actual validated arguments,
results and source versions to the receipt and deterministic source-rooted replay.
Internal reasoning, full planner responses, credentials and private user material
are excluded. Only the approved public export is uploaded, never the private
capture directory or SDK envelopes.

Hand-authored, zero-provider native capacity probes admit planning/final requests
of 31,171/18,342 bytes (flute), 30,475/35,505 (sail), and 31,213/35,276 (conveyor).
The latter final requests have narrow margins. Actual model choices may exceed
them; the unchanged guard must reject such a result without truncating source or
raising the ceiling. These probes show expressibility and capacity only. This
exposed three-case regression has no Base arm and does not establish general
reliability, efficacy, production readiness or completion of formal I02–I06.
