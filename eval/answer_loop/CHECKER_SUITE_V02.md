# HCL v0.3 Answer Checker Suite v0.2 — Fresh

Created after the first checker run was observed.

12 fresh cases test:
- explicit fact contradiction;
- information access;
- world truth vs false belief;
- premature collapse of hidden motives;
- calibrated ambiguity;
- over-uncertainty;
- answer granularity;
- unsupported invention;
- absence of evidence vs evidence of absence;
- explicit second-order evidence bridges.

The gate uses:
1. expected PASS/REVISE status;
2. for REVISE cases, overlap with at least one expected violation family;
3. HCL final-check PASS after revision.

Exact lexical wording is advisory only.
